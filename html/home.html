An Entity is an AI-driven creature in the game that behaves like a living being. The term "mob" is short for "mobile entity". All entities can be damaged — whether by falling, being attacked by a player or another entity, getting hit by an arrow, or falling into the void. They also move on their own thanks to their AI.

Different types of entities have unique behaviors and can drop various kinds of loot — some valuable, others less so — depending on which entity was defeated.
